Setting Up the Customer Screen

Method 1 — Second Monitor

  1. Connect a second monitor to this computer with a cable.
  2. Set your display to "Extend", not "Duplicate/Mirror" — on Windows: Display Settings → Multiple displays → Extend. On Mac: Displays → uncheck Mirror Displays.
  3. Copy the link above, open it in a new browser window here on this computer.
  4. Drag that window onto the second monitor and make it full-screen.
  5. Angle the monitor to face your customer.

Method 2 — Tablet or TV (Screen Mirroring)

Important: the tablet needs to mirror this computer's screen. Opening the link directly in the tablet's own browser won't show live updates — it only syncs between windows on this same computer.

  1. First, follow Method 1 above to open the Customer Screen in its own window here on this computer.
  2. Mirror that window to your tablet or TV — Windows: press Win+K, or Settings → System → Display → "Connect to a wireless display." Mac: Control Center → Screen Mirroring (AirPlay).
  3. Make the mirrored window full-screen. Your tablet or TV now shows a live copy of it.

Before You Download

How the offline version works

  • Unzip it anywhere, then run the included launcher — start-server.bat (Windows), start-server-mac.command (Mac), or start-server.sh (Linux) — to open the app in your browser. This is what makes the Customer Screen and camera barcode scanner work reliably.
  • For a single register with no Customer Screen, you can also just double-click app.html directly.
  • It works exactly like this website, offline — same cart, receipts, inventory, and languages.
  • Data is saved only on that computer, with no cloud sync — back up regularly from Settings → Backup.

Getting updates later

Come back and download again anytime while your Premium is active to get the latest version. To carry your data over: on the old copy, go to Settings → Backup → Download Full Backup, then on the new copy use Settings → Backup → Restore from Backup to load it back in.
