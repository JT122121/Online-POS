Free Online POS System for Small Businesses

A simple POS system for businesses that just want to start selling.

GoOnlinePOS is an independently developed free online POS system built for small businesses.

We created GoOnlinePOS around a simple idea: getting a point-of-sale system should not require a monthly subscription, expensive POS hardware, a complicated installation, or a sales call before you can use it.

If you run a market stall, home bakery, food cart, pop-up shop, small independent store, or a new business, you may simply need a reliable way to manage products, record sales, accept different payment methods, and create receipts.

GoOnlinePOS.com was created to fill that gap. It is a browser-based POS system designed to keep everyday selling simple.

No signup. No login. No monthly fee. Open the app, enter your store details, add your products, and start making sales.

GoOnlinePOS works directly in your web browser. You can use it on a phone, tablet, or laptop without installing traditional POS software or buying dedicated checkout hardware.

GoOnlinePOS focuses on the practical tools a small business needs when making everyday sales.

How GoOnlinePOS works

Getting started is straightforward. Open GoOnlinePOS, enter your store details, add your products and prices, then use the sales screen to build your transaction.

When a customer is ready to pay, select the appropriate payment method and complete the sale. You can then create or print the receipt and keep the transaction in your sales records.

The goal is not to turn a simple sale into a complicated software process. The goal is to give a small business the essential POS tools it needs and let the business get on with selling.

Why choose a browser-based POS?

A browser-based point-of-sale system can be useful for small businesses because it removes some of the barriers associated with traditional POS installations.

Instead of setting up a dedicated checkout computer or installing large POS software packages, GoOnlinePOS can be opened through a modern web browser. This makes it practical for businesses that sell from different locations or simply want a lightweight way to record sales.

Whether you are selling products at a market, running a small shop, taking orders from a food business, or starting your first business, the basic idea remains the same: open the POS, add your products, make the sale and keep the record.
