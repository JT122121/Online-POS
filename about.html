Why we built this

Not every business can afford a POS system. That's the whole point of this one.

GoOnlinePOS is an independently developed project created for small businesses.

Most point-of-sale software is built for businesses that already have money to spend - a monthly subscription, a card reader, sometimes a whole checkout counter's worth of hardware, and a sales call before you can even try it. If you're running a market stall, a home bakery, a food cart, or just starting out with barely enough stock to fill a shelf, none of that is really built for you.

GoOnlinePOS.com exists for exactly that gap. It's for the business that can't afford a "proper" POS system, and just as much for the one that could afford it but doesn't want the complexity, the contracts, or the polish that comes bundled with it whether you asked for it or not.

No signup. No login. No monthly fee. No sales call. Open the page, add your products, and start ringing up sales - that's the whole onboarding process. It runs entirely in your browser, so there's nothing to install and nothing standing between you and your first sale.

GoOnlinePOS focuses on the essential tools a small business needs - take payments, print receipts, and keep sales records - without unnecessary complexity.
