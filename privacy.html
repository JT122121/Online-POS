Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Overview

GoOnlinePOS.com ("we," "our," "the app") is a free, browser-based point of sale tool. There is no signup and no user account. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

Information we do not collect

We do not operate a server that stores your business data. Everything you enter - product names and prices, cart contents, sales history, store details, your logo, and receipt settings - is saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. We never receive, see, or have access to any of it.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics to understand overall traffic to the site (for example, how many people visit and roughly where from). This may collect:

  • Pages viewed and time spent on the site
  • Approximate location, derived from IP address (typically country or city level)
  • Browser type, device type, and referring site

Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. You can find out more about how Google handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

The app loads a small number of resources from third-party providers to function:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) - for the typefaces used on the page
  • cdnjs.cloudflare.com - for the library that powers the "Download Backup"
  • Google Analytics - for basic traffic statistics, as described above

These providers may log requests (including IP address) according to their own privacy policies, independent of us.

Your data can be lost - please back it up

Because your products, sales history, and settings are stored only in your browser, clearing your browser's site data, switching browsers or devices, or reinstalling your browser will permanently delete them. We have no way to recover this information. We recommend regularly using the "Download Sales Report" button in Settings to keep your own copy of your sales records.

Cookies

Aside from cookies set by Google Analytics, the app itself does not set cookies. Your browser's local storage (used to save your products and settings) is not a cookie and is not sent to any server.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can clear your browser's site data at any time to remove everything saved by the app. You can also use browser settings, extensions, or Google's opt-out tools to limit Google Analytics tracking.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact form.
