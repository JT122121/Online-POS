Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

Overview

GoOnlinePOS.com ("we," "our," "the app") is a free, browser-based point of sale tool. There is no signup and no user account. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

Information we do not collect

We do not operate a server that stores your business data. Everything you enter - product names and prices, cart contents, sales history, store details, your logo, and receipt settings - is saved only in your own browser's local storage, on your own device. We never receive, see, or have access to any of it.

Information collected automatically

We use Google Analytics to understand overall traffic to the site (for example, how many people visit and roughly where from). This may collect:

  • Pages viewed and time spent on the site
  • Approximate location, derived from IP address (typically country or city level)
  • Browser type, device type, and referring site

Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. You can find out more about how Google handles this data in Google's Privacy Policy.

Advertising

This site uses Google AdSense and may show ads served by it. Google, as a third-party vendor, and its advertising partners use cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this site or other sites on the internet. This allows Google and its partners to serve ads that may be more relevant to you.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info. Opting out doesn't remove ads from the site - it just makes them less tailored to you.

Third-party services

The app loads a small number of resources from third-party providers to function:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com) - for the typefaces used on the page
  • cdnjs.cloudflare.com - for the library that powers the "Download Sales Report"
  • Google Analytics - for basic traffic statistics, as described above
  • Google AdSense - may show ads, as described above

These providers may log requests (including IP address) according to their own privacy policies, independent of us.

Your data can be lost - please back it up

Because your products, sales history, and settings are stored only in your browser, clearing your browser's site data, switching browsers or devices, or reinstalling your browser will permanently delete them. We have no way to recover this information. We recommend regularly using the "Download Full Backup" button in Settings to keep your own copy of your data.

Cookies

Aside from cookies set by Google Analytics and Google AdSense, the app itself does not set cookies. Your browser's local storage (used to save your products and settings) is not a cookie and is not sent to any server.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not knowingly show personalized ads to children.

Your choices

You can clear your browser's site data at any time to remove everything saved by the app. The cookie banner on this site (and the "Cookie Settings" link in the footer) lets you decline analytics and advertising cookies entirely - the site works exactly the same either way. You can also use browser settings, extensions, or Google's opt-out tools (see "Advertising" above) to limit tracking.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through our contact form.
