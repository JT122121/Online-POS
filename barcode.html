How to create a barcode or QR code

  1. Choose Barcode or QR Code at the top.
  2. Pick a label size from the list, or choose Custom and enter your own size in millimeters.
  3. Type the value you want to encode. For a barcode, also pick the barcode type that matches what you're printing for - for example EAN-13 or UPC-A for retail products, CODE128 for general use.
  4. Optionally add text above the code (e.g. a product name), and choose whether to show the encoded value underneath it.
  5. For a QR code, you can also add a small logo image or a short piece of text to the center - a high error-correction level is used automatically so it still scans correctly.
  6. Click "Save" to keep it in your Saved Codes list, "Download PNG" to save an image file, or "Print / Save as PDF" to print it directly.

Barcode / QR Code FAQ

Is this barcode/QR code generator free?
Yes. It runs entirely in your browser - no signup, no account, and nothing is uploaded anywhere.
Where can I see my saved codes?
Click "Save" to add the current one to your list, then click "Saved Codes" any time to browse, reopen, or delete them. They're stored only in this browser - clearing your browser data removes them.
Will my barcode scan correctly?
Most scanners read CODE128, EAN-13, UPC-A, and similar standard formats reliably. Make sure your value matches the chosen format's requirements (shown as a hint if it doesn't), and avoid printing the label too small.
Can I add my logo to a QR code?
Yes - turn on "Add an image or text in the center" under QR Code and upload an image, or add a short piece of text instead. A high error-correction level keeps the code scannable even with part of the center covered.
What sizes can I print at?
Pick a standard label size, or choose Custom and enter any size in millimeters - the on-screen preview and the printed output match exactly.
