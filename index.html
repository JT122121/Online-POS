How it works

Four steps, no account required. Everything is saved right in your browser as you go.

01

Add your products

Type items in one by one, or upload a CSV or Excel file with names, prices, and categories to load your whole catalog at once.

02

Ring up a sale

Tap products to build the cart, or add a custom item on the spot. The total updates live on the receipt preview.

03

Take payment & print

Pick a payment method, enter the amount received, and print. A new sale starts automatically right after.

04

Track your sales

Every completed sale is saved automatically. Download a full Excel report from Settings whenever you need it.

Set it up your way

Everything lives in Settings - tap the gear icon in the top right.

Upload your products

Add items one by one, or upload a CSV or Excel file with names, prices, categories, and stock levels in Settings → Products to load your whole catalog at once.

Search & filter products

Find items instantly with live search, or tap a category chip to filter your catalog down to what you need.

Your logo & store details

Upload your own logo and store info in Settings → Store - it prints at the top of every receipt.

Scan barcodes

Turn on the barcode scanner and add items instantly by scanning with any USB barcode scanner - no camera, no setup, it just works.

Split payment

Part cash, part card? Add multiple payment rows on one sale and the app tracks each amount separately.

Multiple cashiers

Add your team in Settings → Cashiers, switch who's active from the header, and see sales broken down by cashier in the Summary panel.

Stock & inventory

Track stock levels per product - they deduct automatically with every sale, and you can correct or restock any number anytime from the Inventory tab.

Customer-facing screen

Show customers a live view of their order and total on a second monitor or tablet - updates in real time as you ring things up.

Tax & discount

Set a tax name and rate in Settings → Tax. Discounts are applied per sale at checkout, as a percent or a flat amount - totals recalculate automatically either way.

Multiple currencies

Set your currency code and symbol in Settings → Currency, and choose how many decimal places to show.

Six languages

Switch the whole interface - English, Arabic, Filipino, Hindi, Spanish, or Thai - from Settings → Language.

Sales history, editable

Click any past sale to view full details, fix a mistake, or reprint the receipt. Export a two-tab Excel report whenever you need your own records.

End of Day report

Print or save a closing report from the header's End of Day button - total sales, items sold, payment breakdown, and collections per cashier for the day.

Full backup & restore

Since everything lives in your browser, download a complete backup anytime from Settings → Backup - products, sales, logo, and all - and restore it instantly if you ever need to.

About GoOnlinePOS

GoOnlinePOS is an independently developed free online POS system built for small businesses.

We built it around a simple idea: running a POS shouldn't require a monthly subscription, expensive hardware, a complicated setup, or a sales call.

If you run a market stall, home bakery, food cart, pop-up shop, or small independent store, you need a reliable way to manage products, record sales, accept payments, and print receipts - nothing more.

GoOnlinePOS.com was created to fill that gap. It's a browser-based POS you can use on a phone, tablet, or laptop - no traditional POS software to install, no dedicated checkout hardware to buy.

Beyond the POS itself, GoOnlinePOS has grown into a small suite of free business tools - invoicing, receipts, pricing, VAT, and barcodes - each usable on its own, right in your browser, with nothing to sign up for. See More Free Tools for Your Business above.

Common questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The basic GoOnlinePOS plan is free forever, with no subscription, signup, or login required. Signing in with Google also gets you a free 15-day Premium trial automatically - no code needed. After that, Premium is a paid add-on (starting at $3.99/month, with discounted 3-month and annual plans) that unlocks extras like Company Logo, Customer Screen, and Inventory editing, or redeem a code anytime to extend it.

Do I need to create an account?

Not for the free Basic plan - just open the app in your browser and start selling. Signing in with Google is only needed for Premium, which starts with a free 15-day trial automatically - no code required to begin.

Can I use the POS without internet?

Yes. GoOnlinePOS can be used without an internet connection. The Offline POS lets you continue selling, manage products, and use your POS even when there is no internet.

Where is my data stored?

Your products, sales history, logo, and settings are stored locally in your browser. Your POS data is not uploaded to a GoOnlinePOS server. Because your data is stored locally, clearing your browser's site data can erase it, so regular backups are recommended.

Can I backup and restore my data?

Yes. Go to Settings → Backup to download a complete backup of your POS data. You can restore the backup later to recover the data contained in that backup.

Can I export my sales?

Yes. Export a two-tab Excel report of your sales and inventory anytime from Settings → Sales History.

Can I use it on my phone, tablet, or computer?

Yes. GoOnlinePOS works in modern web browsers on phones, tablets, laptops, and desktop computers. The interface automatically adjusts to different screen sizes.

Can I print receipts?

Yes. GoOnlinePOS supports receipt printing from any printer connected to your device. Leave the paper size on Auto and it will fit the available print area, or choose your exact paper size in Settings → Paper & Zoom for more precise results.

What payment methods can I use?

You can accept Cash, Card, Bank Transfer, and other payment methods that you can add and customize for your business.

Can I set my own tax or VAT?

Yes. You can customize the tax name and tax rate to match your business requirements.

Can I change the currency?

Yes. You can change the currency used by the POS and customize the number of decimal places.

Is Premium a subscription?

Yes. Premium follows your signed-in account rather than being a one-time unlock on a single device - redeem a code to extend it for 30, 90, or 365 days depending on the plan (1 Month, 3 Months, or 1 Year), and it works on any device you sign in on. Redeeming another code while you're already Premium stacks the extra time on top of what you have left, so redeeming early never costs you anything.

What do I get with Premium?

Premium unlocks additional features such as the customer screen, logo customization, advanced inventory features, editable receipt numbering, an ad-free experience, and access to the Offline POS version.
