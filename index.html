How it works

Four steps, no account required. Everything is saved right in your browser as you go.

01

Add your products

Type items in one by one, or upload a CSV with names, prices, and categories to load your whole catalog at once.

02

Ring up a sale

Tap products to build the cart, or add a custom item on the spot. The total updates live on the receipt preview.

03

Take payment & print

Pick a payment method, enter the amount received, and print. A new sale starts automatically right after.

04

Track your sales

Every completed sale is saved automatically. Download a full Excel report from Settings whenever you need it.

Set it up your way

Everything lives in Settings — tap the gear icon in the top right.

Upload your products

Add items one by one, or upload a CSV with names, prices, categories, and stock levels in Settings → Products to load your whole catalog at once.

Your logo & store details

Upload your own logo and store info in Settings → Store — it prints at the top of every receipt.

Scan barcodes

Turn on the barcode scanner and add items using your device's camera — no separate scanner hardware needed.

Split payment

Part cash, part card? Add multiple payment rows on one sale and the app tracks each amount separately.

Multiple cashiers

Add your team in Settings → Cashiers, switch who's active from the header, and see sales broken down by cashier in the Summary panel.

Stock & inventory

Track stock levels per product — they deduct automatically with every sale, and you can correct or restock any number anytime from the Inventory tab.

Customer-facing screen

Show customers a live view of their order and total on a second monitor or tablet — updates in real time as you ring things up.

Tax & discount

Set a tax name and rate, plus a discount percentage, in Settings → Tax & Discount. Totals recalculate automatically.

Six languages

Switch the whole interface — English, Arabic, Filipino, Hindi, Spanish, or Thai — from Settings → Language.

Sales history, editable

Click any past sale to view full details, fix a mistake, or reprint the receipt. Export a two-tab Excel report whenever you need your own records.

Full backup & restore

Since everything lives in your browser, download a complete backup anytime from Settings → Backup — products, sales, logo, and all — and restore it instantly if you ever need to.

Every setting, explained

A full walkthrough of the app — from your Store details down to Premium — with real screenshots, not stock photos.

MAIN SCREEN

Your everyday screen

This is what you'll spend most of your time on — your product catalog on the left, live receipt preview on the right. Tap a product to add it to the cart, search or filter by category, and use the header buttons to reach Backup, Settings, Summary, Print, Inventory, Customer Screen, and New Sale.

HEADER → CUSTOMER SCREEN
What your customer sees, live
Getting the link takes one click

Customer Screen

Show customers exactly what they're ordering and what they owe, on a second monitor or tablet facing them — items and totals update live as you ring things up, and it shows a "Thank you" screen with the payment breakdown once the sale completes.

Customer Screen is a Premium feature
SETTINGS → STORE

Store

Set your store name and details — address, phone, email, website, anything you want printed at the top of every receipt. Upload your own logo here too, so receipts look like they came from your actual business.

Logo upload is a Premium feature
SETTINGS → DOCUMENT

Document

Choose whether you're printing a Receipt, Invoice, or Payment Received. The current receipt number lives here too — editable, with a custom prefix if you want one (like INV-000123).

Editable receipt number & prefix are Premium features
SETTINGS → TAX & DISCOUNT

Tax & Discount

Name your tax (VAT, GST, Sales Tax — whatever applies to you), set the rate, and add a discount percentage if you run promotions. Every total recalculates automatically.

SETTINGS → PAYMENT METHODS

Payment Methods

Cash, Card, and Bank Transfer come built in, and you can add your own — GCash, PayPal, whatever your customers actually use. These show up in the dropdown at checkout, including for split payments.

SETTINGS → CASHIERS

Cashiers

Add everyone who works the till. Whoever's selected in the header dropdown gets printed on every receipt and tracked separately in the Sales Summary panel — so you always know who rang up what.

SETTINGS → PRODUCTS

Products

Add items one at a time — name, price, category, SKU, and stock level — or upload a CSV to load your whole catalog in one shot. Product names are editable right in this list, and changes sync everywhere instantly, including Inventory.

SETTINGS → SALES HISTORY

Sales History

Every completed sale is saved automatically, grouped by date. Click any sale to view full details, fix a mistake, or reprint the receipt — and download a two-tab Excel report whenever you need your own records.

SETTINGS → FOOTER

Footer

The two lines that print at the bottom of every receipt — "Thank you for shopping with us!" by default, but make it your own.

SETTINGS → PAPER & ZOOM

Paper & Zoom

Leave it on Auto to fit whatever printer you're using, or pick 58mm, 80mm, A5, A4, or a custom width. Screen zoom adjusts how large everything looks while you work.

SETTINGS → CURRENCY

Currency

Set your currency code and symbol, and choose how many decimal places to show — 2 for most currencies, 3 if you're working in something like Bahraini Dinar.

SETTINGS → LANGUAGE

Language

Switch the entire interface between English, Arabic, Filipino, Hindi, Spanish, or Thai — including the Customer Screen, which picks up the same language automatically.

SETTINGS → INVENTORY

Inventory

Stock is grouped by category, with SKU and total value shown per item. Numbers deduct automatically with every sale — edit any figure directly to correct it or restock, and download a full inventory report to Excel whenever you need it.

Editing stock & exporting reports are Premium features
SETTINGS → BACKUP

Backup & Restore

Since everything lives only in this browser, back it up regularly — one click downloads products, sales history, settings, your logo, and more into a single file. Restoring is just as simple if you ever switch devices.

SETTINGS → BACKUP → OFFLINE VERSION

Offline Version

Download a complete copy of the app that runs with zero internet — a shop computer with no connection, a spare install, or just a backup plan. It's built fresh from the live site each time you click Download, so it's always current, and it includes the Customer Screen and camera barcode scanner. Come back and download again anytime your Premium is active to grab the latest version; carry your data over with Backup → Download Full Backup on the old copy, then Restore from Backup on the new one. See how offline mode works for the full setup guide.

Downloading the offline version is a Premium feature
SETTINGS → PREMIUM

Premium

Premium features stay visible throughout the app with a gold badge, even before you activate anything — you can see exactly what's available. Enter a code here to unlock them on your device.

Common questions

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no signup and no login. Just open the page and start adding products.

Where is my data stored?

Everything — products, sales history, your logo, and settings — is saved locally in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server. That also means clearing your browser's site data will erase it, so use Settings → Backup to download a full backup regularly. If anything's ever lost, restoring that same file brings everything back exactly as it was.

Will it work with my printer?

Yes. Leave the paper size on Auto and it will fit whatever's connected, or pick your printer's exact size in Settings → Paper & Zoom for a precise fit.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes — it works in any modern mobile or desktop browser, and the layout adjusts to fit your screen.

Is it really free?

Yes, completely. No trial period, no subscription, no hidden fees.
