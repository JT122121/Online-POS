How to make an invoice

  1. Add your logo (optional) and fill in who the invoice is from and who it's billed to.
  2. Add an invoice number, date, due date, and payment terms.
  3. List each line item with a quantity and rate - the amount is calculated for you. Check "Tax Free" on any item that shouldn't be taxed.
  4. Add tax and a discount if they apply, then check the balance due.
  5. Click "Save Invoice" to keep it in your Saved Invoices list, and Print / Save as PDF whenever you're ready to send it.

Invoice FAQ

Is this invoice generator free?
Yes. It runs entirely in your browser - no signup, no account, and no data leaves your device.
Where can I see my saved invoices?
Click "Save Invoice" to add the current one to your list, then click "Saved Invoices" any time to browse, reopen, or delete them. They're stored only in this browser - clearing your browser data removes them.
How do I save it as a PDF?
Click "Print / Save as PDF" and pick "Save as PDF" (or "Microsoft Print to PDF") as the destination in the print dialog your browser opens.
Is this the same as GoOnlinePOS's receipts?
No. Receipts are for point-of-sale checkout inside the POS app. This tool is for one-off invoices you send to a customer before or after the sale.
